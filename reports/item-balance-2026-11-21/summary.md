# Item balance report: 21 November 2026

## What was played

500 bot matches on test copies of the server: 8 Expert bots, free-for-all, 4 lives, an item every 3 seconds, random kits, every map in turn (normal and Reverse).

- 250 matches at the real drop rates: [real-drops.md](real-drops.md).
- 250 matches with every item equally likely to drop, so the rare items were picked up often enough to judge: [equal-drops.md](equal-drops.md).

No match had to be stopped for running long. 20,383 items dropped, 14,078 were picked up, and there were 15,433 deaths.

## The short answer

- **Items are a side dish at the real drop rates.** They dealt 7% of all damage and made 6% of the kills (463 of 7,683). Melee alone was 46% of the damage. With every item dropping equally often, items reach 20% of the damage and 18% of the kills, so rarity is what holds them back.
- **Most tiers are in the right order. Legendary is the odd one.** A typical attack item deals 144% of a health bar per pickup at Ultimate, 34% at Epic, 16% at Rare and 4% at Common. Legendary has one item far above that line (Missile Battery, 65%) and two far below it (Rain Staff and Air Strike, 10% each, less than most Rares).
- **Stronger than their tier:** Falling Anvil, Missile Battery, Monster Egg, Shadow Walk.
- **Weaker than their tier:** Rocket Launcher, Air Strike, Rain Staff, Cluster Grenade, Frag Grenade.
- **Four of these are about the bots as much as the items.** Bots never hit the Monster Egg's clone back, lose a Shadow Walker completely, land two Rain Staff projectiles in 120, and place a Crafty Bomb on one pickup in three.
- **Only Shadow Walk clearly changes who wins.** For everything else, holding one item barely moves the result of an 8-player match.

## Stronger than their tier

**Falling Anvil (Ultimate).** 304% of a health bar per pickup and 176 kills per 100 pickups: twice the next Ultimates (Flying Car 152%, Personal Rocket 144%). It hits every opponent, and an 8-player free-for-all has seven of them; it landed on about 3.6 a time. A 4v4 has four opponents, so at the same hit rate it would deal about 175% there: a little above the other Ultimates, not double.

**Missile Battery (Legendary).** 65% per pickup and 58 kills per 100. That is more than the Rocket Launcher, an Ultimate (53%), and more than six times the tier's other two attack items.

**Monster Egg (Uncommon).** 33% per pickup and 22 kills per 100: level with the Epic attack items (Fire Flower 32%, Singularity Nuke 36%) and above every Rare. Because it is Uncommon it drops often, which makes it the item that kills the most at the real drop rates: 55 of the 463 item kills. Bots never attack the clone, though, and it has only a fifth of a health bar: a person would swat it.

**Shadow Walk (Legendary).** Its holder won 37% of their matches, against 19% for the holder of an average item. Only 12% of its users were dead ten seconds after using it, where 40 to 55% is usual. Bots drop a hidden fighter from their targets completely; a person would keep swinging at where they last were.

## Weaker than their tier

**Rocket Launcher (Ultimate).** 53% per pickup, about a third of what is usual for the tier. It has one shot, and on 46% of pickups it hit nobody. It also costs its user 7% of a health bar.

**Air Strike (Legendary).** It hit someone on one pickup in six, for 10% per pickup, and hurt its own user half as much (4.7%). Three seconds of warning is enough for an Expert bot to walk out of it.

**Rain Staff (Legendary).** 10% per pickup. It holds 125 volleys; a bot gets through 21 of them, and of those 120-odd projectiles about two land. Its holder won 12% of their matches, the lowest of all 41 items, and 75% of its holders were dead within 20 seconds of picking it up (65% is usual). These figures do not say why: it may be the bots' aim with it, or that using it leaves them open.

**Cluster Grenade (Uncommon) and Frag Grenade (Common).** Each hit someone about one time in ten, for 3.3% and 1.8% per pickup. The Frag does half as much to its thrower (0.9%) as to everyone else.

**Crafty Bomb (Rare): the bots, not the item.** 6% per pickup, the least of the Rare attack items, but only because it was placed on one pickup in three. When it is placed it averages about 19% a use, which is in line with the tier.

## Other things the numbers show

- **Anything on a fuse mostly misses.** Thrown items that wait two or three seconds hit someone on 10 to 36% of pickups (Cluster 10%, Frag 11%, Crafty Bomb 12%, Air Strike 17%, Proximity Mine 26%, Singularity Grenade 32%, Singularity Nuke 36%). Most items that fire straight away hit on 80 to 96%.
- **The singularities bite their user.** The Singularity Nuke costs its user 12.3% of a health bar per pickup, a third of the 36% it deals to others; the Singularity Grenade 9.4% against 22.3%.
- **Healing.** Awesome Soup gave back 59% of a health bar, the Gilded Apple 50% (as absorption), Steak 11.4% and Porkchop 10.4%. The First Aid Kit gave 7.9% at once, less than the Common foods, with its twenty seconds of regeneration on top (not counted).
- **Items that sit unused.** Pocket Trampoline and Cloud in a Bottle were used on about a third of pickups and the Instant Teleporter on 40%: bots keep them for getting back to the map. A fighter holding one cannot pick up another item until it is gone.
- **Knocking people off the map works.** The Player Grapple made 33 kills per 100 pickups and the Knockback Rod 14, neither dealing any damage.
- **Cage Trap.** Its holders won 13% of their matches, against 17% for the other Uncommon utility items. Its users did not die any sooner after throwing it than users of other items (44% within ten seconds), so these figures do not show the trap catching its own owner.

## What I would change

In the items:

1. **Missile Battery: 16 rockets down to 10.** A rocket averages 5.3% and bots fire about three in four, so roughly 65% down to 40% per pickup: still the best attack item in its tier, but under the Ultimates.
2. **Rocket Launcher: a bigger blast,** so a near miss still counts. It is an Ultimate that hits nobody almost half the time.
3. **Air Strike: two seconds of warning instead of three.**
4. **Frag and Cluster Grenades: shorter fuses** (Frag two seconds to one and a half, Cluster three to two).
5. **Falling Anvil: leave it at 85% for 4v4.** It is only double the other Ultimates in a big free-for-all.

In the bots, then measure again before touching the item:

6. **Monster Egg:** a bot hits back at a clone that is on it.
7. **Shadow Walk:** a bot keeps attacking where a hidden fighter was last seen.
8. **Rain Staff:** watch a bot use it and find out why so little lands, before deciding it is the item.
9. **Crafty Bomb:** an Expert bot waits for an opponent to stand still before placing it, and mostly never gets the chance; let it place the bomb on anyone close.

## How far to trust this

- **Bots played every fighter.** Each figure is the item in an Expert bot's hands, against Expert bots. An item the bots use badly looks weaker than it is, and one they cannot answer looks stronger.
- **Free-for-all with eight, not 4v4.** Items that reach every opponent (Falling Anvil) or work best in a crowd count for more here than they would in a team match.
- **One item rarely decides a match.** Shadow Walk (above the average) and Rain Staff and Cage Trap (below it) are the only three whose lift is clear of chance in both runs. Personal Rocket and the Shovel upgrade, both at +7, are suggestive and no more; the Shovel's is mixed up with which kits have a shovel.
- **Not counted:** poison (so the Poison Mist shows only that it reached someone on 30% of pickups), the damage of the tool upgrades, Armor Upgrade and Mystery Ability (it is counted as the kit's), and regeneration over time.
- **Few pickups at the real rates.** Each Ultimate was picked up 19 to 26 times in the real-rate run; their figures come almost entirely from the equal-drop run.
