# Item balance after the bot fixes: every item dropping equally often

250 matches (250 with a winner, 0 stopped for going on too long), 8.0 fighters each, 2:04 on average.
10244 items dropped, 7077 were picked up (69%).
Items dealt 19% of all damage; kits (abilities and melee) dealt the rest.
7752 deaths: 1383 by an item (18%), 6158 by a kit, 211 that were nobody's kill.
The holder of an average item won 20% of the matches they had it in: that is what "Lift" is measured from.

Damage, healing and self-damage are in percent of a full health bar, per pickup. Kills are per 100 pickups.
Lift is how much more (or less) often than that average the item's holder won, in points, give or take the figure after it;
a lift smaller than twice that figure is as likely chance as the item.

## What these figures leave out

- Bots played every fighter, so each figure is the item in a bot's hands: an item the bots use badly looks weaker than it is, and one they cannot answer looks stronger.
- A tool upgrade becomes the upgrade for a tool the fighter's kit has. A dropped Shovel upgrade can be picked up as an Axe upgrade, so read the three together.
- The tool upgrades, the Armor Upgrade and the Mystery Ability do their damage through the kit's own abilities, where it is counted as the kit's: only Lift says anything about them.
- Poison is not counted as damage, so the Poison Mist shows who it reached but not what it did to them.
- Healing is what the item gave back at once: the First Aid Kit's twenty seconds of regeneration are not in its figure, and the Gilded Apple's is its absorption hearts.
