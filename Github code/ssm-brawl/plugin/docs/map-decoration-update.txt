SSM AI Edition — scaled plants and miniature tree trunks
Installed 2026-11-10

- BIG plants are emitted once per original plant part as centered 2x BlockDisplay
  models. The resampled terrain contains air there, not eight copied plant blocks.
  Flowers, grass, tall flowers/ferns, mushrooms, saplings and crops with empty
  collision shapes are supported, along with sugar cane. Solid/functional
  decorations are not indiscriminately converted into non-colliding displays.
- Tall plants share a root so both visual halves are removed together. Managed
  displays follow chunk loading/unloading and are deleted with the arena.
- Unsupported plants are removed after their underlying 2x2 ground patch is gone.
  Smash terrain destruction also clears plant displays in its affected area.
- SMALL single-column upright tree logs become wood-textured wall-width posts.
  Oak, spruce, birch, jungle, acacia, dark oak, mangrove, cherry, pale oak,
  crimson and warped materials are supported. Thick trunks, horizontal beams,
  stripped wood and mixed masonry retain ordinary terrain conversion.
- The posts use vanilla wall collision: half-block width/depth, with native wall
  collision height. Their custom models use bark sides and log end grain.
  Leaves in small maps are persistent so replacing the logs cannot decay canopies.
- Resource pack adds 11 post models and overrides only standalone, dry, upright
  post states of 11 wall types absent from the current arena/lobby template
  palettes. Connected/waterlogged states retain vanilla models. Ordinary
  cobblestone/mossy/stone-brick walls are not replaced. Reserved post physics is
  held stable in small arenas, so neighbouring blocks do not grow stone arms.
