SSM AI Edition - enlarged map details and Chicken Flap
2026-11-10

Enlarged maps
- Carpets, rails, trapdoors and ladders now have one enlarged display per original block.
  The old eight-cell repetition produced floating carpets/rails and duplicate trapdoors/ladders.
- Carpets and trapdoors enlarge along their surfaces while retaining vanilla thickness.
  Hidden native blocks give those surfaces matching client/server collision. Open and closed
  trapdoors, both halves and all facings are covered. Ladders have climbable native carriers
  only along the supporting wall, with a single enlarged ladder model in front.
- Rail displays preserve straight, curved and ascending shapes. They are decorative arena
  tracks; they do not provide a new minecart system.
- Models stream with their chunks, clean up with destroyed supports/Smash terrain, and release
  tracking on unload. Unused pale moss carpet, aged weeping vines and waxed weathered copper
  trapdoor states provide invisible carriers through the existing resource pack. The source
  arena/lobby palette audit found none of those materials in the imported templates.
- Lower grass layers become dirt. Grass under an opaque full block becomes dirt as well;
  exposed grass and lawns under glass/leaves remain grass.
- Original templates and the editable lobby are unchanged. New matches rebuild disposable copies.

Chicken Flap
- Ground recharge is 60 game ticks (3 seconds at 20 TPS) from empty to genuinely full.
- Removed the 0.9999 cap that prevented the eighth flap from a supposedly full bar.
- The last affordable flap consumes any unusable remainder, so exhaustion shows exactly zero.
- Unlimited-flap Smash powers retain their existing behavior.

Validation
- Gradle test and shadowJar successful; 52 tests, zero failures.
- Isolated Paper 26.3 integration: 1,525 thin-block states; carpet/trapdoor collision compared
  with rendered geometry; all ladder directions climbable; single rail models.
- Twelve-detail terrain fixture across all six reverse/reflect combinations, loaded entities,
  buried/exposed/glass-covered grass, support cleanup, Smash cleanup, native physics protection.
- An actual test player climbed the scaled ladder and stood on the scaled trapdoor surface.
- Chicken: full at tick 60, eight flaps, zero at exhaustion and after a partial final flap.
- Existing 4,183 slab/stair checks, 18 map transformations, giant plants, miniature tree posts
  and a full BIG + Reflect Left + Reverse match passed. /hub still awards no win.
- Resource-pack model references and all 26 weeping-vine model states validated.
- No manual visual inspection in the Minecraft client was performed for this update.
